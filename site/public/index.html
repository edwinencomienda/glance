⌘⇥

Built to stay out of your way.

Every window, not every app

One tile per window, most recently used first. Two Chrome windows? Two tiles.

Instant tap

A quick ⌘⇥ jumps straight to your previous window without drawing anything.

Live thumbnails

Captured with ScreenCaptureKit and cached, so the panel opens with the last frame and updates in place.

Every Space, every display

Windows on all Spaces, including full screen apps. Pick one and Glance takes you there.

Drag to switch

Dragging a file? Press ⌘⇥, hover a window to bring it forward, then drop into it.

Zero idle cost

No timers, no polling. The window list builds in about 5 ms, and a hung app can't freeze it.

Feels like home.

Prefer to keep the macOS switcher? Use ⌥⇥ instead.

Private by design.

Glance never reads what you type. It needs Accessibility to handle ⌘⇥ and Screen Recording for thumbnails, and the only request it makes is a daily check for a new release on GitHub. It never downloads or installs anything by itself.
